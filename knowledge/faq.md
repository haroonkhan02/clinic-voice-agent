# Riverside Family Clinic: front-desk knowledge base

Synthetic knowledge base for the voice agent. The clinic, staff and contact details are
fictional. Each `##` section is one retrievable passage. Keep answers short enough to read
aloud on a phone call.

## Opening hours
We are open Monday, Tuesday and Thursday from 8:00 to 18:00, Wednesday from 8:00 to 13:00 and
Friday from 8:00 to 15:00. We are closed on Saturdays, Sundays and public holidays. No
appointments start during the lunch break from 12:30 to 13:30, but the phone line stays open.

## Address and directions
The clinic is at 12 Example Street, 10115 Berlin, on the ground floor next to the pharmacy. The
nearest underground station is Example Platz, about three minutes on foot. Buses 142 and 245 stop
right outside.

## Parking
There is no private car park. Paid street parking is available on Example Street and there is a
public garage two blocks away on Market Road. One accessible parking space is reserved in front of
the entrance for patients with a disabled parking permit.

## Accessibility
The clinic is step-free from the street, has an accessible toilet and a wheelchair-accessible
examination room. Guide and assistance dogs are welcome. If you need extra time or help during
your visit, tell us when you book.

## Languages spoken
Our team speaks German and English. Dr. Okafor also speaks French and Nurse Lin also speaks
Mandarin. For other languages you are welcome to bring a family member or we can book a
telephone interpreter if you tell us when you book.

## Doctors and staff
Our general practitioners are Dr. Anna Weber and Dr. Daniel Okafor. Nurse Mei Lin runs
vaccinations and blood tests. You can ask for a specific doctor when you book, but the first
available appointment may be with either doctor.

## Registering as a new patient
New patients are welcome. Please arrive ten minutes early for your first appointment and bring
your health insurance card, a photo ID and a list of any medicines you take. If you have recent
test results or letters from other doctors, bring those too.

## What to bring to an appointment
Bring your health insurance card at every first visit of the quarter, a list of your current
medicines and any referral letter. For children, bring the child's vaccination record.

## Appointment types and length
A general consultation is 15 minutes and a long consultation is 30 minutes. Book a long
consultation if you want to discuss more than one problem. Vaccinations and blood tests are
15-minute appointments with Nurse Lin.

## Blood test preparation
Blood tests take place in the morning, with the last appointment at 10:00. For a fasting blood
test, do not eat for 10 to 12 hours before the appointment. Water is fine. Keep taking your usual
medicines unless your doctor told you otherwise. If you are unsure, ask your doctor; the front
desk cannot advise on medicines.

## Vaccinations
Nurse Lin gives routine vaccinations such as flu, tetanus and travel vaccines. Bring your
vaccination record. For travel vaccines, book at least six weeks before you travel. Whether a
vaccine is right for you is decided by the doctor or nurse at the appointment.

## Cancelling or rescheduling
Please cancel or move your appointment at least 24 hours in advance so someone else can have the
slot. You can do this by phone at any time. Repeated missed appointments without notice may mean
we ask you to book in person.

## Running late
If you are more than 10 minutes late we may need to give you a new appointment, because the next
patient is already waiting. Please call us if you know you will be late.

## Test results
For privacy reasons the front desk cannot give test results over the phone. The doctor will call
you if anything needs action, or you can book a short appointment or phone consultation to go
through your results.

## Repeat prescriptions
Repeat prescriptions for regular medicines can be requested by phone or in person and are usually
ready to collect after two working days. New medicines or dose changes need an appointment with a
doctor.

## Sick notes
A sick note (certificate of incapacity for work) needs a consultation with a doctor, in person or
by video. We cannot issue sick notes over the phone through the front desk.

## Referrals to specialists
Referrals to specialists are written by the doctor during a consultation. If you already discussed
a referral with your doctor, we can tell you whether the letter is ready to collect.

## Medical records
You can request a copy of your medical records in writing at the front desk or by post. We
prepare copies within one month. Bring a photo ID when you collect them.

## Video consultations
Short follow-up appointments can be done by video if the doctor agrees. Book like a normal
appointment and ask for a video consultation; you will receive a link by text message.

## Home visits
Home visits are only for registered patients who cannot leave home because of illness or
disability. Requests must come in before 10:00 so the doctor can plan the route.

## Fever or infection symptoms
If you have a fever, a new cough or a rash that might be contagious, please call before you come
in. We will give you a time when the waiting room is quiet or offer a video consultation.

## Insurance and payment
We accept all statutory health insurance funds and private insurance. Some services, such as
certain travel vaccines and medical certificates for sport, are not covered by statutory
insurance and are paid on the day by card.

## Out-of-hours care
When the clinic is closed, call the non-emergency on-call medical service on 116117. In an
emergency, such as chest pain, difficulty breathing or heavy bleeding, call 112 immediately.

## Leaving a message for a doctor
The front desk can take a message for a doctor or nurse. Give your name, date of birth and a
callback number. Non-urgent messages are answered within two working days.

## Contacting the clinic
You can reach the front desk by phone during opening hours. For non-urgent questions you can also
write to the clinic by post. We do not give medical advice by email.
