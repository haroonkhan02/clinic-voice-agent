# Where the voice-to-voice time goes

Generated by `cva-plots` from `speech_roundtrip.json` and `phone_sim.json`.

Flux end-of-turn delay (end of speech → `EndOfTurn`) on the labelled caller lines, by guardrail label:

| Label | Median delay (s) | n |
|---|---|---|
| emergency | 1.01 | 25 |
| medical_advice | 0.51 | 20 |
| ok | 0.68 | 30 |

Simulated calls: the same line's Flux delay in the speech eval and its voice-to-voice time in the phone simulation correlate with Pearson r = 0.85 (n = 20). The median remainder (voice-to-voice minus end-of-turn delay: gate, TTS first audio, transport) is 0.07 s. The two numbers come from separate Flux streams on different days, so the remainder is only a rough estimate; the correlation is the robust part.
